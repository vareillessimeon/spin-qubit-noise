# spin-qubit-noise
 
Decoherence, benchmarking and error correction models for silicon spin qubits. Plain NumPy.
 
References: [SpinPulse, arXiv:2601.10435](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10435) · [Gravier *et al.*, arXiv:2507.08713](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08713) · Paz *et al.*, IEDM (device numbers).
 
### Part 1 — Decoherence under 1/f noise
 
Pure dephasing of a single spin, H = δω(t)σ_z/2. Quasi-static, white and 1/f noise generated by spectral synthesis, parameterised by the spectral intensity S₀.
 
- Gaussian-fit residual: 0.038 quasi-static, 0.186 white. The decay law is set by the noise correlation, not its amplitude distribution.
- T₂\* falls from 701 to 436 ns as the acquisition time goes from 0.1 to 102 ms at fixed S₀.
- Hahn echo gain: exact cancellation for quasi-static noise, 4.5 for 1/f.
- Two-component model, 0.3% of the variance in 1/f charge noise: T₂\* = 505 ns, T₂ᵉᶜʰᵒ = 43.1 µs. Measured: 500 ns, 40 µs.
### Part 2 — Randomized benchmarking
 
Clifford RB on the same noise model, one noise sample per gate. Analytic reference for uncorrelated noise, p = (1+2C)/3, gives p = 0.9716 (F = 98.58%).
 
| noise | p | F | SPAM |
|---|---|---|---|
| white | 0.9719 | 98.59% | +0.0011 |
| 1/f | 0.9819 | 99.09% | +0.0185 |
| quasi-static | 0.9922 | 99.61% | +0.0564 |
 
- Correlated errors make the decay a sum of exponentials rather than one.
- The standard fit then overstates the fidelity and reports a nonzero SPAM, although the model has none.
### Part 3 — Distance-3 phase-flip code
 
Repetition code against Z errors. Continuous rotations digitized by the syndrome measurement.
 
- Recovers p_L = 3p²(1−p) + p³ and the break-even point p = 1/2.
- At fixed physical error rate, p_L/p² grows from 2.82 (uncorrelated) to 8.17 (fully spatially correlated), following 3(1+2c²).
### Limitations
 
Classical noise, no T₁, no SPAM, instantaneous pulses, single round with perfect syndrome extraction, no two-qubit gates.
